Root-Cause Anatomy: Why "Sufficient Employees on Paper" Miss Deadlines

1. Structural Skill Mismatch

Infosys maintains over 1,000+ bench engineers in legacy mainframe, manual testing, and Java 7/8 maintenance pools. Incoming digital transformation client deals demand GenAI, Databricks, and Cloud-Native Microservices where usable bench is under 3%.

2. Temporal Availability Latency

Senior certified practitioners in modern stacks are locked into long-term billing engagements with 60-120 day rolling releases, while incoming Fortune 500 contracts enforce strict 14 to 28-day staffing SLAs before contractual penalty clauses kick in.

3. Unverified Proficiency Gaps

Resumes list keyword skills without validated hands-on proficiency (Level 1 Novice vs Level 4 Lead Architect). Junior engineers placed without bridging support fail client technical screenings, deferring project kickoff.
